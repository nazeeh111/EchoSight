## Load-bearing physical conventions

- Metres, right-handed coordinates, z up. Surveyed acoustic centers/uncertainties are supplied calibration, never inferred room structure.
- Plane n·x=d; virtual source q=s+2(d−n·s)n. Excess source-buffer delay is (|r−q|−|r−s|)/(c/kappa). Never halve bistatic delays. Relative receiver/source clocks do not identify absolute physical source rate.
- Joint source/effective-speed covariance replaces separate source/speed/rate budgets, including cross terms. Reused receiver survey groups share errors. Point output uses actual point-model nuisance covariance through the fixed-comparison-weight estimator sandwich.
- A support mesh does not establish physical edges, closed space, empty space or safety. Unseen parent planes and compact scattering locations are competing conditional models, not recovered objects or authenticated geometry.
- Frozen truth never enters fitting. Measured RIR plus generated probe is hybrid replay, not our phone recording. Conditional covariance excludes wrong physics/selection/systematic transducer bias.
