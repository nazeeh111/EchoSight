## Implemented interpretation check

`echosight/path_alternatives.py` is called by `infer_scene_bundle` only after its source-diversity and existing recovered-parent checks. It does not change peak extraction, plane proposals, selected candidate IDs, existing hypotheses, API routes or frozen acceptance files. Each challenged plane has one selected candidate per recording. Alternatives are mutually exclusive explanations of those observations, not extra simultaneous surfaces. All support records are required, with at least four distinct receiver positions at every supplied source.

The comparison uses the complete source/effective-speed covariance, shared receiver survey covariance, independent candidate timing, direct-reference timing and relative-clock terms, frozen at the original plane. The three-parameter plane is refitted to that exact objective; alternatives must score no worse than the smaller original/refitted score, have RMS residual at most 100 microseconds, maximum residual at most 200 microseconds and every residual within three marginal standard deviations. These are engineering compatibility bounds, not probabilities; nonlinear search and candidate selection are not statistically calibrated.

The compact model is `(norm(s-p)+norm(p-r)-norm(s-r))/v`. It searches 32 geometry-derived starts, each with at most 100 evaluations in a 30 m source-relative box. All admissible separated minima survive as `location_modes`. `unique_conditional_mode` requires one returned mode and a rank-three local Jacobian. Rank-deficient or search-boundary-limited modes have null covariance; a pseudoinverse must not portray unconstrained directions as finite certainty. Coplanar mirror modes remain distinct even when each mode has full local rank. Size, shape, material phase and object identity are unestablished.

The missing-parent model first estimates a virtual source per source pose from the selected bistatic ranges, uses proper orthogonal Procrustes for initialization, then fits a five-coordinate rotation about an axis: three rotation-vector coordinates and two axis-perpendicular center coordinates. The center-along-axis gauge is eliminated explicitly. The search uses three starts, at most 100 evaluations each, and ±20 m axis-center coordinates. It checks 180 decompositions into two planes, with both reflection orders and actual bounded ray-segment intersections/reflection geometry for every selected record. An operator with a low residual alone is insufficient. The exported `physical_example` is one member of an unobserved parent family; no hypothetical parent is appended to recovered walls. Finite extent and occlusion remain unknown.

At most ten planes with 64 selected records each are examined. Every numerical optimization and physical-path loop checks cancellation. Results apply atomically after all searches, and a cancelled joint result has no definitive surfaces. The output retains original challenged surfaces under `challenged_first_order_path_interpretations`, adds separate `compact_scattering_location` / `unobserved_two_reflection_family` hypotheses, and preserves earlier hypotheses. Unchallenged surfaces stay definitive only under the existing conditional model semantics. Search is bounded and global optimality is not proven.

## Coordinator corrections: covariance and post-fit errors

The score comparison still freezes the plane-model delay covariance. It is **not** reused as the physical point-model error covariance. Each exported location mode now rebuilds `C_point` from its own compact-path derivatives `Js=(a-u)/v`, `Jr=(b+u)/v`, `Jv=-mu/v`, preserving the complete source/source and source/speed covariance, reused receiver poses, direct timing and relative-clock terms. With `W=C_fixed^-1`, the executed fixed-weight estimator has local influence `L=(Jp.T W Jp)^-1 Jp.T W`; its mode-specific output covariance is the sandwich `L C_point L.T`. This is first-order propagation conditional on that mode, selected paths and the point model. It is not the smaller covariance of an unexecuted reweighted estimator, nor calibrated physical coverage. Rank-deficient or boundary-limited modes still report null covariance.

An independent regression constructs a finite-difference Jacobian over every source, effective speed and reused receiver-pose nuisance, supplies a positive-semidefinite calibration with explicit source/speed cross-correlation, and compares both `C_point` and the estimator sandwich against direct matrix formulas. It verifies that dropping cross terms changes the result and that the former fixed-plane curvature covariance differs. Exported mode fields are checked against the resulting sandwich, while acceptance scores remain unchanged.

A post-fit `ValueError`, `KeyError`, `TypeError` or numerical linear-algebra failure now clears definitive surfaces before returning `calibration_needed`. Any already retained hypothesis is marked `verification_status=unverified_due_to_processing_error`. A regression corrupts receiver grouping specifically during the post-fit stage and verifies that no definitive geometry leaks from the earlier fit.
