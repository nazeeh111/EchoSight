# Physical correctness audit

This is a baseline research prototype. Passing software tests does not qualify the MacBook source or any iPhone. Audit scope: the raw-waveform path, clocks, direct-path identification, waveform/model mismatch, and physical acceptance. The full charter matrix is maintained by the coordinator; the entries below are evidence-specific, not a completion percentage.

## Improvement integrated: rate-matched acquisition retry

The initial nominal-rate chirp can have multiple correlation lobes when the incoming chirp is stretched. Picking successive lobes inconsistently caused rejection even for a perfectly affine clock. From `u = alpha*t + b`, the receiver-grid template is `x(n/(F_receiver*alpha))`. The retry uses the initial bounded slope to form this stretched template, reacquires one peak within one millisecond of each coarse prediction, then refits the line. It retains the retry only if its maximum residual improves. The original 100-microsecond / two-sample residual gate and ±5000 ppm bound still apply. Successful original acquisitions are unchanged. `clock.acquisition_template_rate_refined` reports the retry.

The development comparison varied nine slopes from −4900 to +4900 ppm and three added-noise levels. It compared identical waveforms through the original and improved detector. Thirty negative controls covered nonaffine warp, missing probe windows and pure noise. This is a small deterministic development study, not a frozen independent validation or a physical clock survey. The independent harder suite may uncover additional failure modes.

Reproduce the equal-input comparison from the repository root:

```sh
mkdir -p work/physics
git show 565769a078b3f834de626a5d0697c21f5ceb5e8a:echosight/signals.py > work/physics/baseline-signals.py
.venv/bin/python -m evaluation.physics_development --baseline-signals work/physics/baseline-signals.py --output work/physics/clock-development.json
.venv/bin/python -m unittest tests.test_signals
```

Baseline and current source SHA-256 hashes are recorded in the comparison output. The two frozen regression reports also pin their full executed source hashes; they are regression replays of already-seen held-out data, not newly blind evaluations. The external report uses five measured RIR files convolved with the generated probe, retains 21 accepted and four rejected observations at the default 220-millisecond interval, and produces no definitive geometry because the receivers are collinear. These are hybrid recordings, not our microphones. One accepted observation in the difficult source case has a 5.58-millisecond coordinate-model direct-arrival discrepancy: accepted timing does not establish the direct path. Before this change, the default report had 20 accepted observations (19 from other cases plus this suspect observation); the historical one-second-spacing report had 20 accepted observations from other cases and all five difficult channels rejected. Earlier prose conflated those two reports; this audit corrects that claim. The [new one-second regression](../../evidence/audit-physics/clock-external-spacing-regression.json) still accepts those 20 and rejects all five difficult channels.

## Rejected waveform guard, preserved rather than concealed

A local signed-waveform check compared each peak with a scaled measured direct kernel. The first version used a ±200-microsecond window and excluded paths with more than half their waveform energy unexplained. Alongside a conservative peak-neighborhood guard, it suppressed the development sidelobes and dispersive path. It also rejected legitimate multi-path superpositions: frozen room 101 fell to three matched surfaces against a minimum of four. [`rejected-shape-200us-frozen-regression.json`](../../evidence/audit-physics/rejected-shape-200us-frozen-regression.json) retains the failure.

A narrower `1 / bandwidth` nominal LFM autocorrelation-mainlobe radius was derived to avoid including adjacent paths. That correction still failed the same frozen requirement, including height support; [`rejected-shape-mainlobe-frozen-regression.json`](../../evidence/audit-physics/rejected-shape-mainlobe-frozen-regression.json) retains it. The acceptance criteria were never changed. The final attempted implementation is saved as [`rejected-shape-experiment.patch`](../../evidence/audit-physics/rejected-shape-experiment.patch). **Neither guard is in the runtime.** Do not cite its development successes as a shipped fix or infer waveform accuracy from a small residual.

## Exact own-device qualification experiment

These are proposed engineering acceptance gates frozen before our first device data. They are not published accuracy or current successes. Raw capture, configuration, independent measurements and rejected trials must all remain in the acceptance package. If these targets are unattainable, document the failure and revise the scenario explicitly rather than silently widen thresholds.

1. **Acquisition continuity and losslessness.** At each of 3–4 phones, record silence and ten stationary shots. Export the original samples, actual constant sample rate, start/stop/interruption/route metadata available from the recorder, device/OS/recorder version and exact probe manifest. Require ten complete seven-pilot shots, zero reported interruption/route/rate changes, zero malformed samples, no clipping diagnostic and affine residual ≤100 microseconds (or two delivered samples, whichever is larger). A failure disqualifies that acquisition route until the cause is fixed. Pilot success alone does not prove sample continuity; compare recorder export behavior against the native recorder fallback if app timestamps are unavailable.
2. **Gain and repeatability.** Survey one fixed source/receiver pair and one large planar reflector. Capture three shots each at three fixed, nonclipping source gains spanning at least 12 dB. Keep physical geometry, channel route and receiver gain processing settings fixed. For a clearly separated reference echo require the median excess delay to vary by ≤100 microseconds across gain and repeat shots. If it fails, current linear waveform timing is unqualified; lower the level, change band/route or qualify a recorder that disables processing. Do not hide bias by inflating the isolated-path scatter.
3. **Source model and calibration.** Use at least 12 independently surveyed microphone stops spanning at least three bearings and three heights, with a repeated reference stop. Freeze eight calibration stops and four held-out stops before fitting. Survey the reference reflector independently; keep its truth out of room mapping inputs. Compare measured excess delay with the actual source–reflector–receiver model, allowing an estimated effective source center and effective `c / kappa` only on the eight calibration stops. Require held-out RMS excess-delay residual ≤100 microseconds and maximum ≤200 microseconds; no systematic bearing/height trend larger than 100 microseconds. If it fails, the single point-source model / band / acoustic-center assumption is not qualified. Repeat left/right route and orientation qualification separately; never transfer a pass automatically.
4. **Clock behavior.** Repeat the same geometry across all phones at both the 220-millisecond and one-second pilot periods, at least three shots per phone and interval. Require the same maximum affine residual gate and gain-stable excess delays. The inferred alpha is relative to source playback, not an independent measurement of either physical clock. A source-rate or effective-speed scale calibrated on the reference plane remains a common calibration, not proof of each clock's absolute accuracy. Nonaffine failure requires reacquisition or a richer independently validated timing model.
5. **First physical mapping gate.** After the above pass, record twelve noncoplanar stops with the source fixed. Freeze independent wall/floor/ceiling surveys outside fitting. Require at least four independent recovered structural surfaces including a horizontal surface, offset error ≤10 cm and normal error ≤5 degrees, and zero definitive unmatched surfaces under that same matching tolerance. Report every missed surveyed surface and every withheld-pose response prediction error. Test a direct-only/null arrangement where feasible and do not count missing echoes as empty space. Failure means the chosen demonstration is not qualified, even if a rendered result is attractive.
6. **Finite reflector / change gate.** Only after a static pass, use A→B→A with the same recording stops and a surveyed movable large reflector. Reserve a repeat fixed reference phone to detect route/source drift. Require a localized change supported at three or more noncoplanar stops with repeated excess delays agreeing within 100 microseconds; require the repeated A estimate to return within the established geometric tolerance. An observed waveform change without adequate spatial support remains unlocalized. Smaller objects, hidden edges and general mesh recovery remain unsupported.

The geometry tolerance above is an initial demonstrator requirement, not a guarantee that a 100-microsecond path residual always gives 10 cm plane accuracy: receiver geometry and ill-conditioning still matter. Any result that fails its observability checks stays partial or ambiguous regardless of average residual.

## Sources and inference depth

This audit derives the acquisition retry directly from the declared affine time mapping and tests it in executable code. It adds no claim of novelty. Existing source access and exact dataset versions remain in [`SOURCES.md`](../SOURCES.md) and [`SIGNAL_MODEL.md`](../SIGNAL_MODEL.md). Published laboratory RIRs establish that real reverberant waveforms were exercised; they do not establish consumer-device clocks, source centers, lossless app behavior or room accuracy on our hardware.
