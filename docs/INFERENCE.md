# Inference and geometry, version 1

The backend estimates multiple **unclassified planar reflectors**, including slanted surfaces and horizontal surfaces whose height follows acoustic travel times. Surveyed source and receiver acoustic-center positions are inputs; room geometry is not. A useful configuration uses four phones in two stationary, surveyed arrangements with varied heights. A single fixed source and at least four distinct receivers are required; seven independent supporting views are required for definitive geometry; four to six yield unconfirmed hypotheses. Device count and placement count differ.

## Forward model and unknowns

Positions use metres, right-handed coordinates and z up. A plane is `n·x=d`, with unit normal `n`. Source `s` reflects to image source `q=s+2(d-n·s)n`. At receiver `r`, direct distance is `D=|r-s|` and reflected distance `L=|r-q|`. The observable excess **source-buffer** delay is `(L-D)/v` with `v=c/kappa`, physical sound speed `c`, and actual/nominal source sample rate `kappa`. Physical delay is this delay divided by `kappa`. The solver never blindly halves excess travel distance. Signal processing must correct receiver/source relative clock slope before producing these observations; this does not establish absolute physical sample rate.

Unknowns are three image-source coordinates per reflector plus exclusive associations from echo candidates to reflectors. Source/receiver positions, sound speed and source rate are externally declared calibration with uncertainty, not unconstrained fitted degrees of freedom. Each image source determines a plane through the midpoint of `s,q`, normal to `q-s`. Code: `geometry.image_source`, `plane_from_image`, `excess_delay`. Reflection points intersect the receiver-to-image segment with that plane; source and receiver must lie on the same side and the intersection must fall strictly inside the segment.

## Search and serious alternative

`infer_scene` uses the intersection of three spheres centered at receiver positions, with radii `D+v*t` for unlabeled candidate delays. Both sphere intersections are retained. Two broad, mostly disjoint receiver triples and up to eight local triples seed the search. Local triples matter for an interior reflector visible only from one side; maximum-aperture triples can all straddle it. Residual-aware ranking balances coherence and support count rather than always promoting loose many-view matches. Every proposal is scored against **original nonlinear excess delays** at every view. Linear range differences are never accepted as fit evidence. Coarse ranking and robust least-squares refinement propose individual reflectors; greedy complete-model additions then enforce one-to-one candidate use with Hungarian assignment. A detected peak can support only one final reflector at that capture. Declared merged peaks are excluded. Missed observations and unmatched clutter remain possible, and no reflector is returned unless its complete model improves upon the null model.

`infer_baseline` is the serious alternative: direct-plane hypotheses from 99 deterministic directions (96 approximately uniform hemisphere directions plus three axis directions) and both exact one-echo plane-offset roots, then identical refinement and complete-model scoring. This makes initializer coverage, time and false correspondences directly comparable on identical observations. It has no Manhattan-only restriction. `infer_first_echo` is the simple baseline: keep only the earliest echo per recording and run physical bistatic fitting, making its wrong-correspondence failure visible without using an incorrect propagation law.

Search is deliberately bounded: 32 views, 18 candidates/view, 24,000 proposals, 72 refinements, 35 function evaluations/refinement stage, 10 surfaces. Limits are diagnostics; cap-limited results are partial. These are search heuristics, not globally exhaustive enumeration or proof of uniqueness. Callback cancellation is checked between batches and solver calls. Progress callback receives `(fraction, message)`.

## Complete-model score and uncertainty

Exclusive assignment uses a fixed diagonal initializer scale of 25 mm and a 75 mm residual gate, clutter penalty 5, miss penalty 1, plane penalty 7. A definitive plane needs seven distinct assigned captures. Four-view initial fits only generate candidate hypotheses. This deliberately conservative confirmation policy was chosen after development clutter exposed false four- and six-view correspondences; it is not a universal false-positive guarantee. The complete model is ranked with

`0.5 eᵀΣ⁻¹e + sum(log(diag(cholesky(Σ))/sigma_ref)) + m/2 log(2π) + miss + 5 clutter + 7 planes`,

where `sigma_ref=100 us`. This dimensionless ranking is **not a posterior probability**. The covariance includes independent detector variance; each capture's common direct-path timing error and relative clock-slope uncertainty; per-receiver shared position error across all that receiver's echoes; shared source position error across captures and surfaces; and shared effective-speed error. For `u=(r-s)/D`, `g=(r-q)/L`, `A=I-2nnᵀ`, the pose derivatives of excess length are `g-u` for receiver and `u-A g` for source. Effective-speed derivative is `-delay/v`. These derivatives build correlated covariance, including cross-surface blocks. Cholesky solves score residuals. Local image-source covariance is `(JᵀΣ⁻¹J)^+`, conditional on a fixed association and model. Geometry-dependent covariance is used in the final score; proposal refinement is explicitly approximate and does not optimize that score's determinant.

Surface offset/normal uncertainty and local line-intersection distance uncertainty propagate this local joint covariance. Shared calibration errors therefore do not simply average away as independent echoes. Unmodeled source directivity/multiple drivers, diffuse reflection, finite-source filters, wrong associations and missed global hypotheses remain outside these standard deviations. No calibrated numerical confidence probability is reported.

## Ambiguity, support, dimensions and guidance

Collinear receivers yield an explicit ambiguous result. When the receivers supporting a particular reflector are coplanar, reflecting its image source through their survey plane leaves the reflected ranges unchanged. The fixed source need not lie in that plane. The alternative must satisfy same-side physical reflection checks. This test uses supporting receivers, so an elevated receiver with no matched echo cannot falsely resolve the ambiguity. Both global hypotheses are retained, the definitive surfaces list is empty, status is ambiguous, and a surveyed move out of that plane is suggested. Full local numerical rank would not remove this global ambiguity. Conversely, local rank deficiency must not be hidden by a pseudoinverse: such fits remain candidate hypotheses, and unobservable orientation covariance is returned as null. If the scene is ambiguous, all definitive surfaces move into hypotheses; supported planes invariant under the tested symmetry remain explicitly available there. Four to six total views produce ambiguous candidate-only output because accidental unlabeled correspondences have weaker rejection.

Surface triangles span only the convex hull of inferred reflection points. `extent_status=unknown` and `mesh_semantics=reflection_support_convex_hull_not_physical_edges` prevent treating this polygon as observed wall edges or a closed room. A wardrobe face and a wall can explain the same reflector. Nearly parallel planes (within 3 degrees) provide a **local line-intersection distance**, not a unique global separation. The reference is the surveyed source acoustic center. Align the unit normals, normalize their sum to get direction `w`, and intersect `x=p+t*w` with each plane: `t_i=(d_i-n_i·p)/(n_i·w)`. Report `|t_a-t_b|` together with `reference_point_m`, `direction`, and both `intersection_points_m`. This definition is translation invariant and equals ordinary separation for exactly parallel planes. For tilted planes it changes legitimately with the chosen physical reference; subtracting raw plane offsets is incorrect. An enclosure, room volume and empty/safe space are not asserted. Guidance supplies a suggested surveyed position and its purpose. Unknown audibility prevents guaranteeing that a move will resolve all ambiguity.

Surface IDs hash the sorted supporting capture/candidate IDs, giving deterministic repeatability for the same evidence. IDs can change when a reflector's support set changes; the session comparison layer must track cross-revision associations rather than treating the hash as permanent object identity.

`recommend_next_view(session, result, candidate_positions_m)` ranks supplied placement options by actual predicted bistatic delay separation between competing image-source hypotheses. Its denominator propagates the **difference** of shared source, receiver and effective-speed sensitivities plus independent timing error. Same-plane moves receive zero separation for exact mirror pairs; elevated moves can discriminate them. It does not use scene truth, guarantee audibility, or establish that a proposed position is accessible. Repeated positions within 1 cm count as one independent geometric view.

## Verification

`python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_inference.py` covers original bistatic geometry and reflection point, six independent room planes from unlabeled delays plus clutter, ten random-candidate clutter controls, repeated-pose rejection, off-receiver-plane source mirror ambiguity, unsupported elevated-view ambiguity, active-view discrimination, evidence exclusivity, height/mirror ambiguity, insufficient views/null/cancellation, shared source uncertainty growth, and alternative method output. These are software/synthetic tests, not physical accuracy evidence. End-to-end imported recordings, frozen evaluation, null/mismatch cases, external measured behavior and runtime live in the repository evaluation evidence.

Development raw-waveform evidence after false-correspondence hardening: the initial eight-view room retains four of six true planes, including a horizontal height-dependent reflector; two six-view-supported planes are withheld as candidates. The available clutter development scenario returns no definitive surfaces. Additional surveyed views are the planned information improvement, rather than reducing the confirmation requirement to recover missing surfaces.

After the first checkpoint, twelve-view development recordings recovered six room planes across five seeds. Adding local anchor triples and residual-aware ranking also recovered the tilted interior reflector in development seeds 1 and 5, giving seven independent planes, but missed that reflector in seeds 2–4; those misses are preserved in evaluation. No support threshold was relaxed. The first eight records are byte-identical when extra captures are added. This improvement demonstrates additional acoustic information and better partial-visibility search, not universal object reconstruction. Ten deterministic candidate-null controls produced zero definitive surfaces under the seven-view confirmation policy; this small control set is not a general false-positive bound.

## Review correction: local dimensions and reference uncertainty

Dimension `kind=local_plane_intersection_separation` differentiates the complete line-intersection distance through both image-source vectors, including changes in plane orientation and line direction. The resulting geometry term uses the full shared image-source covariance. Moving the reference `p` with both planes fixed has gradient `n_a/(n_a·w)-n_b/(n_b·w)` (up to sign). Its standard-deviation contribution is `source_position_std_m * norm(gradient)`. Source calibration also affects fitted geometry, so those two contributions cannot be assumed independent. Because their cross-covariance is not separately stored, `std_m = geometry_std_m + reference_std_bound_m` is a conservative first-order standard-deviation bound for arbitrary correlation, **not a calibrated confidence interval**. The reference term vanishes for exactly parallel planes. The usual model/association and unmodeled-bias limitations remain.

The regression uses two planes differing by two degrees, then translates all supplied source/receiver coordinates by `[17,100,-31]` metres without altering the recordings' delays. Both reported distance and uncertainty must remain equal, and the reported endpoints must translate with the frame. This test reproduced the prior origin-dependent offset-subtraction defect before the fix. An exact-parallel regression checks that the reference uncertainty term vanishes.
