# Acquisition and later hardware acceptance

The implemented processing route is a fixed MacBook source and stationary lossless mono recordings at surveyed microphone stops. Three or four phones can collect a placement concurrently; then move them to new stops while holding source position, orientation, routing and gain fixed. The main software demonstration uses eight stops over two placements. One qualified phone may instead visit all stops in a static room. Phones need not start recording together. At least one meaningful height change is essential to test upper/lower ambiguity; an approximately 1 m height span and asymmetric horizontal placement are practical starting targets, not a universal observability guarantee.

Positions refer to the acoustic speaker and microphone apertures, not device centers. Use a tape measure and a common right-handed metre coordinate frame with z upward. Record independent pose uncertainty honestly, along with source model/routing/orientation, phone model/OS, recorder version, actual delivered rate, interruptions, room state and optional measured temperature. Supplied poses are calibration inputs; they are not inferred geometry. Keep independent wall measurements outside session inputs, in evaluation annotations.

## iPhone import route

First qualification uses the free phyphox app's continuous appended audio export, with the custom experiment in `examples/`. This avoids signing a native application or deploying a website. It requests 48 kHz and records its **actual rate output**. Export samples and rate before clearing or starting a new shot. The experiment is statically schema-validated; deployment, export fidelity, audio processing and continuity on our iPhones remain untested.

The official [phyphox audio-input documentation](https://phyphox.org/docs/file-format/input/) was read on 2026-09-18. Its audio section documents appended sample buffers and actual-rate reporting; on iOS the requested rate is only a preference. Our fixture names the samples column `sample_value` and the rate column `reported_rate_Hz`. Use the actual constant reported rate, not a guessed 48 kHz label. Preserve original exported bytes. A CSV import's numeric decoding is not proof the app exported the underlying ADC samples without resampling or processing. No sample timestamps are supplied by this fixture, so sub-pilot discontinuities cannot be ruled out.

A PCM WAV export from another qualified recorder is also accepted. Lossless container encoding does not prove microphone processing was disabled. Do not use a compressed Voice Memos file as calibrated input. If phyphox fails qualification, the next bounded alternative is a minimal native AVAudioSession measurement-mode recorder; deployment/signing is not implemented here. [Apple's measurement-mode entry](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfaudio/avaudiosession/mode-swift.struct/measurement) was reachable but JavaScript prevented extracting detailed API content on this pass; its behavior must be verified against the active SDK before implementing that fallback. Browser capture is also possible but requires a trusted secure origin on each phone; laptop localhost does not make its LAN address secure. Backend completion does not claim either unbuilt fallback.

## Source playback

Generate the probe once and play the entire file as one continuous buffer at its nominal rate. Do not schedule its seven repetitions with separate playback calls or network timing. Preserve the probe manifest and WAV hash through capture. Source-buffer sample rate is not an absolute physical clock calibration; the session carries `source_clock_scale` and `source_clock_std_ppm`. Alpha recovered from the recording is only relative to this source buffer.

For a room with a long reverberation tail, use a 1 s repetition interval (6.32 s buffer) instead of the 220 ms default. This eliminated two pilot-consistency failures across all 25 available measured-response-derived replay signals without relaxing timing thresholds; see `SIGNAL_MODEL.md`. This is a playback-duration tradeoff, not a hardware validation result.

Keep playback gain modest and fixed. Choose a supported channel route and record it. Qualify left-only and right-only playback separately when available; a multi-speaker laptop is not automatically a point source even on one channel. Keep lid angle and orientation fixed. A known source position with centimetre uncertainty requires evidence that an effective acoustic source center is adequate over the chosen band. Path-dependent driver delay can produce false geometry even when the direct path is stable. No sound-pressure safety or inaudibility claim follows from a digital amplitude value.

## Physical acceptance, after the integrated software

1. At one surveyed stationary phone stop with a clear direct line of sight, capture silence and three identical shots. Record route/format/actual rate and preserve originals. Keep the screen active; any interruption or rate/route change invalidates the shot.
2. Repeat at lower playback gain. Require no clipping, seven recovered pilots, acceptable affine residuals, and stable direct and early-reflection delays. Compare level-dependent peak shifts to the declared timing uncertainty; widening a confidence interval is not a cure for arbitrary processing.
3. Repeat at two additional distinct bearings and one elevated position. Verify a target reflector's delayed path moves as the bistatic model predicts. Reserve at least one pose and its wall truth before fitting as a held-out test.
4. Compare source channel routes/orientation. A direction-dependent residual beyond the timing/pose/speed budget challenges the point-source model. Requalify source/band/placement rather than silently labeling a phantom wall.
5. Expand to two four-phone placements only after the first route passes. Keep at least one repeated reference stop to distinguish scene change from device/route drift. Record A→B→A if demonstrating a moved reflector; response change alone is reported as unlocalized until geometric support exists.

Initial timing allocation is about 0.1 ms per isolated path; pose and conditioning may dominate map accuracy. Real acceptance uses independently surveyed geometry, a frozen tolerance based on the actual calibration budget, false/missed surfaces and withheld viewpoints. The synthetic software acceptance in `EVALUATION.md` is a different result. No current hardware accuracy is demonstrated.
