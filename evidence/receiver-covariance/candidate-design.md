# Candidate censoring, chance association, and receiver-array uncertainty

2026-09-19. Design only. No new fitting, no held-out answer tuning. Existing scientific results and failed branches remain authoritative.

## Decision

Correct the independently demonstrated receiver-array covariance omission first. There is not yet evidence that lifting the strongest-18 candidate limit improves spatial inference. Recommend at most one subsequent isolated experiment: uncensored bounded candidate catalog plus **source-held predictive model averaging**, compared with an equal-input direct-plane scorer. Do not promote it unless fresh synthetic recovery and false-surface gates both pass. Do not reopen the closed density or receiver-held guard unchanged.

## What current evidence establishes

At immutable de8442b, the 40 measured dEchorate recordings yield one reference-compatible and two unmatched planes; the frozen wrong-receiver permutation yields four unmatched planes. The strongest-18 limit affects 35/40 recordings. Evaluation reports 78/240 survey-predicted first-order compatibility opportunities after the cap versus 163 before it (85 additional); the echo-refined diagnostic is 89 versus 178. These predictions are model-generated, not independently labeled measured echoes. Larger catalogs necessarily increase accidental matches. Before interpreting any extra compatibility as useful, evaluate the SAME pre/post catalogs against the frozen receiver permutation and report excess compatibility over that same-density control, with source/capture stratification. Neither this control nor a positive excess proves recall or resolves survey bias. Original/refined source locations differ by roughly 9.6–15.5 cm; no covariance widening is authorized to absorb that discrepancy.

The waveform extractor first finds repeatable peaks, then retains the strongest 18; single-source preparation independently caps them again. The image-source proposal search enumerates candidate triples until 24,000 proposals, so a larger catalog alone can starve later anchor combinations. Ranking and exclusive assignment use fixed 25 mm residual scale and constant clutter reward 5, independent of candidate density or search count. Measured pre-cap catalogs range from 8 to 159 candidates, with substantial source-dependent density. A cap-only change is therefore neither a fair information comparison nor a false-association correction.

Closed evidence, not rerun: `work/density-trial/results.json` shows density-scaled clutter reward changed 38 uniform-null false planes to 36, 100 clustered-null false planes to 100, and 60 true room planes plus four false to 60 plus three. The closed recording-held guard (`evidence/audit-physics/recording-guard-trial/README.md`) suppressed tested null errors but lost eight of 25 true twelve-view structures with the original extractor; the joint extractor/guard lost three. FLAIR remained ten missed reference structures. Receiver-list permutation assumes exchangeability, violated by pose-dependent clutter and structured paths. Hidden-parent coherent paths also pass that guard: association significance is not reflection-order identification.

## One bounded candidate experiment, conditional on useful evidence

Keep a bounded catalog of all repeatable detector candidates up to 256 per recording, with overflow explicitly unresolved. No amplitude cutoff beyond the existing detector qualification; no label-dependent rescue. Use four leave-one-source-out folds: train on three source positions with all ten receiver poses, validate on the withheld source. This preserves receiver aperture unlike the closed six-versus-six receiver split. All data-dependent proposal generation, refinement, weights and geometry uncertainty are training-only. Training covariance must condition shared receiver/source/speed nuisance variables, rather than count a reused receiver survey as independent withheld evidence.

Use a deterministic geometry-only normal bank and per-candidate plane-offset voting, with each recording's total vote normalized, to construct a bounded proposal bank without a cubic candidate-prefix loop. Refine no more than 96 proposals per fold. Both the new score and serious competitor receive identical catalogs, folds and proposal/refinement bank; maximum 24,000 provisional bins. This discretization may miss weak or tilted planes and must be measured, not assumed harmless.

For candidate point process `Y_i`, predicted delay `mu_i(theta)`, normalized timing kernel `f_i`, declared detection probability `p`, and independently specified local clutter intensity `lambda_i(t)`, use

`L_i(theta) = (1-p) + p * sum_{t in Y_i} f_i(t-mu_i(theta)) / lambda_i(t)`.

This sums possible candidate assignments instead of selecting whichever one is closest. Given training data, normalized proposal weights `w_h` define withheld predictive score `sum_h w_h product_i L_i(theta_h)`; do not use the maximum over searched hypotheses. Under a correctly specified independent Poisson null this likelihood ratio has conditional mean one, unlike the maximum. Real detector peaks have exclusion spacing, correlated clutter and uncertain intensities, so this is an engineering model-comparison score, not a calibrated probability or universal false-positive bound. Multiple emitted families need joint exclusive reconciliation; a global-null score does not prove each plane in a mixed scene. Shared-nuisance integration, finite visibility, nonuniform intensity estimation and proposal approximation are load-bearing assumptions. Unknown/absent held-source paths cannot claim source-relocation evidence.

Serious equal-input competitor: the same full-catalog proposal bank with the existing exclusive nearest-peak plane score and residual scale. Also retain the unchanged strongest-18 raw-input mapper as a baseline. Freeze a single engineering emission threshold using only new development cases; no measured labels. Failure to specify a defensible nonuniform background without leaking held data is a reason to close the experiment, not fall back to the failed uniform density reward.

Power can be poor: 140 peaks in 80 ms implies about 1,750/s; a 50 us Gaussian kernel has peak density about 7,979/s, so even a perfectly aligned isolated held arrival contributes only a roughly 4.6 density ratio before missed-detection and search-mass penalties. Sparse finite reflectors may remain unrecoverable. The experiment must report those misses, not increase support requirements and call abstention recovery.

## Freeze and stop budget

Before implementation, freeze seven independent synthetic families: easy six-plane room; dense weak six-plane room; finite tilted reflector plus room; clustered diffuse null; receiver-dependent nonstationary null; mixed real planes and coherent higher-order paths; wrong-receiver geometry control. Two development seeds each, followed by four untouched seeds each. Include shared array translation/rotation errors drawn from declared covariance and a separately labeled fixed survey-bias mismatch. Ground truth stays outside proposals and scoring. Keep prior frozen gates unchanged.

Required new gates: all six easy-room surfaces including both height-dependent planes; at least four independent dense-weak room planes including height; at least one useful finite-reflector gain over equal-input competitor across the four fresh cases, with all misses reported; zero false definitive planes across null/wrong-pose cases and zero extra planes in mixed cases; no easy-room regression versus unchanged baseline. Report each case rather than averaging away failures. Timing: at most 60 s and 1 GiB per bundle on the documented local machine. Timeouts/overflow are failures, not discarded cases. Maximum two implementation iterations and one fresh frozen evaluation. If any consequential gate fails, close default promotion. Only after synthetic gates pass may the unchanged measured 40-RIR protocol be rerun by evaluation. No threshold fitting to measured target planes.

## Independently justified covariance correction

Current multi-source plane and path-alternative code correlate measurements only when `receiver_pose_group_id` is identical. Different capsules mounted on one surveyed array can share translation and rotation error. Five capsules with the same projected 20 mm translation uncertainty retain 20 mm uncertainty in their mean; treating five identical marginals as independent reports 8.94 mm. Repeating them across sources does not restore omitted cross-capsule blocks.

For receiver `r_i` on an array centered at `o`, small rigid errors obey `delta r_i = B_i eta + epsilon_i`, `B_i = [I, -skew(r_i-o)]`. Thus `C_R[i,j] = B_i Sigma_array B_j.T + delta_ij D_i`. A one-degree orientation uncertainty at 0.32 m radius contributes about 5.6 mm with structured cross-capsule correlation. The delay covariance is `J_source C_source J_source.T + J_receiver C_R J_receiver.T + C_timing` under explicit source/receiver independence. It can increase or decrease particular contrast uncertainty; no universal inflation factor is valid.

Small coherent contract: ordered full `3G x 3G` receiver-position covariance over at most 64 unique receiver group IDs, explicitly independent of source/effective speed and explicitly replacing scalar receiver terms. Exact group coverage, bounded unique IDs, finite symmetric PSD matrix, stable reused-group positions. Shared groups are represented once even across source sessions. Apply to plane sandwich covariance, parent-path marginal gates, fixed comparison covariance and actual compact-path sandwich covariance. Preserve legacy behavior when absent. Full joint source/receiver/speed covariance would additionally represent survey-frame cross terms; this smaller contract does not, and must declare that limitation. This correction neither fixes biased survey means nor establishes empirical confidence coverage.
